A little about Dad

Life keeps moving, but some things do not change.

I am still working as a Principal, for now, and I still believe I have one of the best jobs in the world. I still love cooking, making things, listening to music, watching rugby league, and spending far too much time experimenting with recipes and other projects in my little laboratory.

I have made many new friends who would love to get to know you one day. And I also want you to always remember that your family in Australia love you very much and would be very happy to hear from you too, whenever you are ready.

I have kept this section deliberately simple. It is here so that, even if you are not ready to write to me, you can still know a little about my life and know that I am thinking of you.

“You never need to earn your way back into my life. You are my daughters. That has never changed.”
